Purpose of the Lab

The purpose of this lab is to introduce you to stealthy reconnaissance operations within a secure, zero-trust edge-computing environment. In this module, you will observe how reconnaissance missions are executed while minimizing detection, communication exposure, and operational footprint.

Goals/Outcomes
  • Learn how reconnaissance missions prioritize minimal visibility, reduced signaling, and controlled communication.
  • Monitor how mission nodes collect environmental and situational data without active engagement or exposure.
  • Examine how limited or intermittent communication affects mission coordination and data flow.
  • Understand how the system adapts when operating in stealth mode versus normal execution.
