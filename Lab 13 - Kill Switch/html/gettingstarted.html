Purpose of the Lab

The purpose of this lab is to introduce you to kill switch mechanisms used to safely terminate mission execution when mission integrity, system security, or operational safety is compromised.

Goals/Outcomes
  • Learn the purpose and importance of kill switch mechanisms in mission-oriented systems.
  • Manually trigger the kill switch and observe immediate mission shutdown and safety enforcement.
  • Analyze how the system automatically terminates missions when communication is lost or mission integrity is threatened.
  • Confirm that drones, processes, and mission data are securely shut down during kill switch enforcement.
