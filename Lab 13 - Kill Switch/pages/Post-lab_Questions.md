# Post-lab Questions

**1. What is the primary purpose of the kill switch mechanism demonstrated in this module?**

a. To improve mission performance and speed
b. To allow drones to continue operating without supervision
c. To safely terminate mission execution when security or integrity is at risk
d. To recover lost communication links automatically

**Correct Answer:** C

**2. In the operator-initiated kill switch scenario, what condition must be true before the kill switch is triggered?**

a. Communication between the GCS and drone must be lost
b. The drone must enter autonomous mode
c. Active communication between the GCS and drone is available
d. The payload must already be deployed

**Correct Answer:** C

**3. What causes the autonomous kill switch to activate during mission execution?**

a. The operator manually clicks the kill switch button
b. The drone completes its mission objectives
c. Communication with the GCS is lost and cannot be re-established
d. The payload deployment is delayed

**Correct Answer:** C

**4. Which behavior confirms that the kill switch has been successfully enforced?**

a. The drone increases altitude and returns to base
b. The mission continues under autonomous control
c. Drone movement and onboard processes are terminated securely
d. The mission map refreshes automatically

**Correct Answer:** C

**5. What message or indicator confirms that a mission has ended due to kill switch enforcement?**

a. Mission Completed Successfully
b. Mission Paused for Review
c. Mission Aborted in Safe Mode
d. Mission Awaiting Operator Input

**Correct Answer:** C
