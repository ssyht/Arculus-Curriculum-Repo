Purpose of the Lab

The purpose of this lab is to demonstrate how replay attacks can compromise encrypted command-and-control systems and how replay protection mechanisms prevent such attacks. In this module, you will extend a UAV command simulation to capture encrypted network traffic and replay previously valid commands to observe how the system behaves with and without freshness validation.

Goals/Outcomes
  • How replay attacks work in encrypted communication systems
  • Why encryption alone does not prevent replay attacks
  • The role of freshness validation in command authentication
