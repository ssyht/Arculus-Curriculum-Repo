Purpose of the Lab

The purpose of this lab is to demonstrate how unsecured command-and-control channels can be exploited in autonomous drone systems when commands are transmitted in plaintext. In this module, you will observe how MAVLink commands sent without encryption or authentication can be intercepted and injected by an adversary, allowing unauthorized control of a UAV during mission execution.

Goals/Outcomes
  • A large-scale wireless testbed that enables experimentation with UAVs, wireless communication, and cyber-physical systems in controlled environments.
  • A lightweight messaging protocol for communication between ground control stations and unmanned vehicles.
  • Reference documentation for MAVLink commands, flight modes, and mission execution behavior used in this lab.
  • A network protocol analyzer used to capture and inspect MAVLink traffic during the experiment.
