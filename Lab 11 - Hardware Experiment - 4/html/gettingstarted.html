Purpose of the Lab

The purpose of this lab is to guide you through the final and most critical stage of securing a Jetson Orin Nano system: hardware fuse programming and secure boot validation. In this module, you will transition from a testable, unfused secure boot configuration to a permanently enforced hardware root of trust.

Goals/Outcomes
  • Official reference for PKC, SBK, fuse programming, and secure boot enforcement on Jetson platforms.
  • NVIDIA-provided flashing tools and bootloader components used to generate and flash signed images.
  • Examine how limited or intermittent communication affects mission coordination and data flow.
  • Background on optional UEFI Secure Boot and how it complements NVIDIA’s hardware root of trust.
