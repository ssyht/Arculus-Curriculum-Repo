Purpose of the Lab

The primary objective of this lab is to provide hands-on experience in understanding how Denial of Service (DoS) conditions impact mission execution and system availability during live operations. Students will observe how excessive or abnormal traffic affects system responsiveness and overall mission behavior within a simulated environment.

Goals/Outcomes
  • Identify how Denial of Service conditions affect system availability and mission execution.
  • Run a mission and introduce a Denial of Service scenario during active execution.
  • Monitor mission responsiveness, system stress, and activity logs during abnormal traffic conditions.
  • Compare baseline system behavior with behavior observed during a DoS condition.
