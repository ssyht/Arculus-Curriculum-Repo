# Assessment Questions/Answers

## Question 1
What event triggers the intermittent connectivity condition during the mission execution?

A. A physical capture of the surveillance drone  
B. A denial of service attack on the ground station  
C. A loss of communication between the ground station and the surveillance drone  
D. A low battery warning on the supply drone  

**Correct Answer:** A loss of communication between the ground station and the surveillance drone

---

## Question 2
When communication loss is detected, what action does the system take to restore connectivity?

A. The mission is immediately aborted  
B. The surveillance drone returns to the ground station  
C. A communication relay drone is automatically deployed  
D. The mission parameters are reconfigured  

**Correct Answer:** A communication relay drone is automatically deployed

---

## Question 3
What additional disruption is simulated while intermittent connectivity is active?

A. Brute force SSH access attempts  
B. Physical capture of a mission asset  
C. GPS spoofing affecting navigation data  
D. Complete shutdown of mission services  

**Correct Answer:** GPS spoofing affecting navigation data

---

## Question 4
How does the surveillance drone respond when GPS spoofing is detected?

A. It continues following the spoofed navigation data  
B. It shuts down immediately  
C. It changes trajectory to follow a safer or pre-planned flight path  
D. t waits for manual operator intervention  

**Correct Answer:** It changes trajectory to follow a safer or pre-planned flight path

---

## Question 5
What is the final outcome of the mission under intermittent connectivity conditions?

A. The mission fails due to loss of communication  
B. The mission is aborted in safe mode  
C. The mission completes successfully despite degraded connectivity  
D. sThe mission pauses indefinitely until connectivity is restored  

**Correct Answer:** The mission completes successfully despite degraded connectivity
