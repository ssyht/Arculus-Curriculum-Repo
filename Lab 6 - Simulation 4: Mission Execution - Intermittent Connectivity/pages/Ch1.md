# Chapter 1

## 1.1 Purpose of the Lab

In this module, you will learn how intermittent network connectivity affects mission execution during live operations. Building on the mission execution framework used in previous scenarios, this lab focuses on observing how temporary communication disruptions impact system behavior, mission progress, and operational awareness.

This lab provides hands-on exposure to intermittent connectivity conditions by allowing you to execute a mission and introduce controlled communication disruptions. The emphasis is on understanding how mission components behave when connectivity is unstable or partially unavailable, rather than implementing recovery or mitigation mechanisms.

By the end of this lab, you will gain practical insight into how intermittent connectivity influences mission reliability and execution in distributed and mission-oriented systems.

## 1.2 Prerequisites

To follow along and get the most out of this module, you should:
* Have access to the provided simulation environment
* Be familiar with navigating the Mission Execution Dashboard
* Understand basic mission execution flow and system monitoring\

## 1.4 Goals / Outcomes

By the end of this lab module, you will be able to:

**(i) Understand Connectivity Disruption Scenarios**
* Identify how intermittent connectivity affects mission execution
* Recognize symptoms of partial or unstable communication

**(ii) Execute Connectivity Disruption Simulations**
* Run a mission under normal connectivity conditions
* Introduce intermittent connectivity during active execution

**(iii) Observe System Behavior**
* Monitor mission behavior during connectivity disruptions
* Analyze activity logs related to communication loss and recovery
