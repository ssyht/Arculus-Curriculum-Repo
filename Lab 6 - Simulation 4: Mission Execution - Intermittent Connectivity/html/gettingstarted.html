Purpose of the Lab

The primary objective of this lab is to provide hands-on experience in understanding how intermittent network connectivity impacts mission execution during live operations. Students will observe how temporary communication loss and unstable connectivity affect mission awareness, system responsiveness, and overall execution behavior within a simulated environment.

Goals/Outcomes
  • Identify how intermittent connectivity affects mission execution and situational awareness.
  • Run a mission and introduce intermittent connectivity during active execution.
  • Monitor mission progress and activity logs during periods of unstable communication.
  • Understand how missions continue operating under partial or unstable network conditions.
