# Chapter 1

## 1.1 Purpose of the Lab

In this module, you will learn how mission execution can be disrupted through Brute Force access attempts and Physical Capture within a simulated operational environment. Building on the pre-configured mission execution framework, this lab focuses on observing how the system responds to adversarial actions targeting authentication mechanisms and physical assets during an active mission.

This lab provides hands-on exposure to two distinct disruption techniques by allowing you to execute a mission and introduce controlled adversarial conditions. The emphasis is on understanding system behavior when faced with unauthorized access attempts and physical compromise of mission components.

By the end of this lab, you will gain practical insight into how mission continuity and control can be affected by brute force attacks and physical capture events in mission-oriented systems.


## 1.2 Prerequisites

To follow along and get the most out of this module, you should:
* Have access to the provided simulation environment
* Be familiar with navigating the Mission Execution Dashboard
* Understand basic mission execution flow and system monitoring
