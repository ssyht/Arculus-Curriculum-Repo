# Chapter 1 - Overview

## 1.1 Purpose of the Lab

In this module, you will learn how to observe and analyze the impact of a Denial of Service (DoS) attack within a simulated system environment. Building on the pre-configured simulation testbed, this lab focuses on understanding how excessive or abnormal traffic affects system availability and responsiveness during live operation.

This lab is designed to provide hands-on exposure to a DoS scenario by allowing you to execute a controlled simulation and monitor system behavior before, during, and after the attack. Rather than implementing defensive mechanisms, the emphasis is on recognizing system stress, performance degradation, and availability failures caused by sustained request overload.

By the end of this lab, you will gain practical experience in identifying DoS-induced service disruption and understanding why availability is a critical concern in cloud, edge, and mission-oriented systems.

## 1.2 Prerequisites

**To follow along and get the most out of this module, you should:**

* Have access to the provided simulation environment
* Be familiar with using a terminal or command-line interface
* Understand basic concepts of system services and request-response behavior

## 1.4 Goals/Outcomes

By the end of this lab module, you will be able to: 

**(i) Understand DoS Impact on System Availability**
* Identify how abnormal traffic patterns affect system performance
* Recognize symptoms of service degradation and reduced responsiveness

**(ii) Execute a DoS Simulation Scenario**
* Run a pre-configured Denial of Service simulation
* Observe system behavior under normal and attack conditions

**(iii) Analyze System Behavior During Attack**
* Compare baseline system behavior with attack-induced behavior
* Understand why availability failures occur under sustained load
