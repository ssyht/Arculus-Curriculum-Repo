# Assessment Questions/Answers

## Question 1 

**In the Denial of Service (DoS) simulation, what is the primary objective of introducing excessive traffic into the mission environment?**

A. To immediately terminate the mission

B. To observe how system availability and responsiveness are affected

C. To reconfigure mission roles dynamically

D. To simulate physical capture of mission assets

**Correct Answer: To observe how system availability and responsiveness are affected**

## Question 2

**Before triggering the Denial of Service simulation, what should be observed to establish a baseline?**

A. Drone battery consumption levels

B. Normal system responsiveness and service behavior

C. Encryption keys used in mission execution

D. Network firewall rule configurations

**Correct Answer: Normal system responsiveness and service behavior**

## Question 3

**Which action initiates the Denial of Service condition during mission execution?**

A. Uploading the mission manifest file

B. Clicking Execute Mission

C. Selecting Simulate Denial of Service from the simulation controls

D. Navigating to the Mission Planning Dashboard

**Correct Answer: Selecting Simulate Denial of Service from the simulation controls**

## Question 4

**During the DoS simulation, which interface component provides real-time insight into system behavior and responses?**

A. Mission Planning Dashboard

B. Activity Log

C. User Management Panel

D. Node Configuration View

**Correct Answer: Activity Log**

## Question 5

**What key system behavior is demonstrated by the Denial of Service simulation?**

A. Automatic recovery without performance impact

B. Improved system throughput under load

C. Degraded responsiveness due to excessive traffic

D. Immediate shutdown of all mission components

**Correct Answer: Degraded responsiveness due to excessive traffic**
